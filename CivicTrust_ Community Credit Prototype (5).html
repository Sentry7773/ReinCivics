CivicTrust: Community Credit Prototype

Interactive prototype. All data is simulated: no real money, no live ledger, no pilot results. Hashes are computed in your browser to show how tamper-evidence works. Run protection, treasury, valuation and inflation are rule-based simulations; predictive validation, a trained treasury agent and live data feeds are planned, not built.

Start here

CivicTrust turns verified pledges of skills, goods and care into spendable local credit, tracks how long that money stays in the community, and protects the fund with rules. This prototype lets you run the whole loop in a few minutes.

What is real in this prototype

Working logic: pledge classes, verification lab (NDVI test, sensor checks, audit statistics, habitat units), stakeholder pack builders, valuation formula, credit and spend, hash-chained audit log, LERI and LM3 arithmetic, treasury rule checks, drift test, run tiers, solvency ladder. Simulated: all people, money, confirmations and market data. Planned: backend and database, identity checks, payment-institution integration, live satellite and sensor feeds, trained treasury agent, notifications, multi-community scale.

Assumptions to confirm before launch

Unit values and quantities per class; haircut mappings where no schedule exists; outcome scores and natural-capital values; run and treasury thresholds; starting fund balances; e-money and payment-law status of any voucher; data-protection basis for biometrics and health data; tax treatment; housing-law detail in the case study.

Glossary

Commons: local credit, 1 = £1. Reserve: savings and emergency funds. LERI: cumulative local activity divided by the amount first issued. LM3: three-round local multiplier. TER: expected shortfall divided by reserve; above 1.0 means the cohort is under-reserved. A-IPS: hard investment limits the treasury cannot override. OMV: open market value. S, E, Q, H: social premium, ecological premium, quality factor, haircut.

Complete journey: twelve months

Ten acts from first contact to a closed regenerative loop. Each act shows its status in this prototype: Working (runs here on simulated data), Simulation (rule-based), Designed, not built, or Pending external (needs partners or real data). All people, amounts and measurements are illustrative.

Numbers cheat sheet

Commons: local credit; 1 Commons = £1. Pledge: a promise of real help, paid only after it is delivered. Haircut: a safety discount because promises can fail. LERI: how many times the same money works locally; 4.0x means £1 became £4 of local activity. TER: worst-case shortfall divided by savings set aside; under 1 is safe, over 1 is not enough savings. DSCR: money available each year divided by the yearly loan payment; 1.26x means £1.26 for every £1 due. NDVI: a 0 to 1 greenness score from satellite images. CO2e: the standard unit for climate impact. Habitat unit: a score for how much nature land supports. CPI: how fast typical prices rise. Hash: a digital fingerprint; change one letter and it no longer matches.

Run protection simulator
Rule-based simulation with illustrative thresholds. Commons cannot be withdrawn as cash; run exposure sits on conversion to RESERVE (1.5% exit fee).


Treasury manager (rule-based simulation)
Seven demo tranches T1 (most liquid) to T7 (least). Yields are assumptions, not forecasts. Constraints: liquidity floor 25% in T1+T2, agent shifts above 5% need human approval. Watcher runs a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test on synthetic returns and engages a kill switch on drift.





Inflation deflector model
Scenario calculator: local CPI = basket-weighted mix of national inflation and inflation on locally sourced goods. Basket weights (food 40, energy 20, housing 25, other 15) are illustrative assumptions. Outputs are scenario results, not observations.
Leontief input-output layer
Why CivicTrust uses it. (1) The basket calculator above treats local sourcing as one share. Real price shocks travel through supply chains: a diesel or fertiliser rise reaches food, transport and housing repairs. The Leontief price model traces that propagation sector by sector, so a deflection claim rests on a transparent mechanism councils and banks can audit. (2) The levers it exposes, local linkage depth and import intensity, are exactly what Vault pledges change. (3) It unifies the metrics: LM3's three rounds are the first terms of the Leontief series I + A + A^2, so the full inverse shows how much multiplier a three-round count leaves out, and LERI, LM3 and deflection share one framework. (4) The ledger already records supplier-to-buyer flows, so real coefficients can be estimated from platform data instead of surveys.




Treasury laws (A-IPS rule engine)
Hard limits taken from the architecture documents, tested against a proposed shift. Fund value (£10M), the starting allocation and the solvency-to-claim-size mapping are illustrative assumptions. No trades are executed anywhere.

Platform status (live, shared state)


Verification lab
Runs the three-stream checks (satellite, sensors, independent audit) and the habitat-unit calculation on simulated inputs. Live imagery, sensor and survey feeds need external connections.


Stakeholder packs
Computes what each institution would receive, from the live prototype state and a synthetic community. Packs are built and hashed here; sending is simulated and recorded in the audit chain.


Stakeholder dashboards
One dashboard per institution, using the same formulas as the CivicTrust metrics API. Inputs are synthetic or illustrative unless a row says it uses live prototype data. Every metric carries its status: BUILT, DESIGNED-NOT-BUILT or PENDING-EXTERNAL.


Voucher system: ten capabilities
Bundles, fractions, swaps, insurance, carbon, biodiversity, expiry, governance, secondary market and open API, running on simulated members. Your own wallet voucher appears after you release a pledge credit and your balance is the real wallet balance. Switch who you act as to try each role.


Compliance centre
Controls, launch gates, monitoring and records for the prototype. Automated tests run against live prototype state. They show control coverage in this prototype, not legal compliance: every legal point below needs counsel to confirm applicability and detail.
